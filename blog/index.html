The rule

DIY Stops Where Gas and Electricity Start

Everything in these guides is outside the equipment: filters, vents, clear space, settings, drains. If a job needs a panel opened, a wire touched, or a gas valve turned, it is not a homeowner job. If you smell gas, leave the home and call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside.

FAQs

Blog Questions

What kind of DIY can a homeowner safely do on HVAC?

Safe homeowner tasks are changing the filter, clearing debris around the outdoor unit, checking vents, flushing the condensate drain with vinegar, and checking thermostat settings and batteries. Gas, electrical and internal-equipment work should be left to a professional.

How often should I do these DIY checks?

Check the filter monthly, clear the outdoor unit monthly in dusty or leafy months, and run the seasonal checklists in spring and fall. A professional cleaning and check once a year covers the rest.

When should I stop DIY and call a professional?

Stop and call (213) 771-9223 if you smell gas, see sparking or water near electrical parts, if a breaker trips repeatedly, or if a problem returns after the basic checks. If you smell gas, leave the home and call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside.
