Privacy Policy

Read how information submitted through the Emergency HVAC LA website may be collected, used, and shared when you contact us about residential heating and air conditioning services.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the information practices for emergencyhvacla.com (the “Website”), operated under the name Emergency HVAC LA. It is written around the contact form and business details currently supplied for this website. The policy should be checked and updated to reflect the actual analytics, cookies, call tracking, hosting, and other vendors in use before publication.

1. Information we may collect

When you submit the contact form, the Website asks for your name, phone number, email address, service requested, service address or ZIP code, and a message describing your residential HVAC inquiry. You choose what to include in the message. Please do not submit payment-card details, passwords, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through the form.

Like many websites, the hosting environment may also process basic technical information needed to deliver and protect the Website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps. The exact server logs and retention periods depend on the hosting provider and its configuration.

2. How information may be used

Information submitted through the contact form may be used to:

  • Review and respond to your service inquiry;
  • Contact you using the phone number or email address you provide;
  • Understand the requested service and check whether the address is within the current service area;
  • Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Website; and
  • Meet applicable legal obligations or respond to valid legal requests.

We do not describe your inquiry as an appointment or confirmed service booking merely because a form has been submitted.

3. Contact form and Formspree

The contact form is configured to submit information to Formspree at formspree.io using the endpoint provided for this Website. Formspree processes form submissions so the inquiry can be delivered to the Website operator. Information you submit is therefore transmitted to and processed by Formspree under its own privacy terms and practices. Review Formspree’s current privacy information before submitting personal information. The Website operator should verify the account’s notification, storage, and retention settings.

4. Cookies and analytics

This policy does not assume that analytics or advertising tools are installed. The Website may use strictly necessary technologies or third-party analytics only if those services are configured. The operator should list the actual tools in use here, explain what they collect, and provide any required consent or opt-out controls. If no analytics or non-essential cookies are used, this section should be updated to say so accurately.

5. Third-party services and links

The Website may rely on service providers to host pages, deliver submitted contact forms, load web fonts, or display an embedded map. For example, the current page design links to Google Maps for directions and uses Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their content. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and settings. The operator should confirm all third-party services actually enabled on the live Website.

6. Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the Website or handle inquiries, where needed for those purposes, or when disclosure is required by law. This policy does not claim that information is sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising; the operator must verify its actual practices and update this statement if they change.

7. Data retention and security

Contact information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, manage related business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Actual retention depends on the Formspree account settings, email systems, and other vendors used. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may choose not to submit the contact form and instead call the business. You may also contact us to ask about personal information you submitted or request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Additional privacy rights may apply depending on where you live and the nature of the information and business. Requests can be sent using the contact details below.

9. California privacy information

California privacy laws may provide certain rights to eligible California residents, depending on whether the business and its data practices fall within the laws’ scope. This page is not a determination that a particular law applies or that the business meets a statutory threshold. If you are a California resident and have a privacy question or request, contact us. The business should obtain legal review of its actual practices and applicable obligations before making specific statutory promises or notices.

10. Children’s privacy

This Website is intended for people seeking information about residential HVAC services and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the Website, contact us so the inquiry can be reviewed.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Website’s features, service providers, or information practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when this text was most recently revised.
