Thermostat Settings That Help Your System Last Longer

You do not need a new thermostat to be kinder to your heating and cooling system. A few steady habits — realistic temperatures, gentle changes, and a thermostat that can actually sense the room — cut strain and surprise breakdowns.

Quick answer

What Is the Best Thermostat Setting for Los Angeles Homes?

A steady 78°F (about 26°C) in summer and 68°F (about 20°C) in winter suits most Los Angeles homes. Avoid swinging the temperature by more than a few degrees at a time: big swings make the system run long, hard cycles, and setting the AC far colder does not cool the home any faster.

The basics

Three Habits That Reduce Wear

  1. Keep temperatures steady. Pick a comfortable setting and leave it there instead of turning the system on and off all day.
  2. Change it gradually. When you come home, adjust by 2–3°F at a time rather than jumping from 85°F to 68°F.
  3. Use a modest setback. When you are away for hours, 4–5°F warmer in summer or cooler in winter is enough. A bigger gap makes the system work hard to catch up.

If your thermostat is old, dusty, or in direct sun, it cannot sense the room properly. Dust it gently with a soft brush and keep lamps and sunlight off it — or ask about a thermostat check during your next service visit.

Batteries

If Your Thermostat Screen Is Blank

Many thermostats run on AA or AAA batteries. If the screen is blank, try fresh batteries first — it is the most common fix and takes a minute. If the screen stays blank with new batteries, check the circuit breaker for the furnace or air handler. If the breaker is fine and the screen is still blank, do not open the thermostat wiring yourself; call (213) 771-9223.

When settings are not the problem

Your Thermostat May Not Be the Issue

If the system runs constantly, rooms are different temperatures, or it short-cycles even at steady settings, the cause is usually airflow, a dirty filter, or a failing part — not the setting. That is the point where a professional diagnostic visit saves more than another round of button-pressing.

Stop and call a professional if…

Do not open the thermostat and touch its wiring, and do not work on the furnace or AC electrical panel. If you see sparking, smell burning, or the breaker trips repeatedly, call (213) 771-9223.

If you smell gas at any point, leave the home immediately and call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside. Do not use light switches or try to fix a gas issue yourself.

FAQs

Thermostat Questions

Does setting the AC colder cool my home faster?

No. The system cools at one speed. Setting it to 60°F only makes it run longer and wastes energy. Set the temperature you actually want.

What temperature should I leave the AC at when I am away?

A setback of 4–5°F from your usual setting is a good balance. A much bigger gap saves little and makes the system work hard to catch up.

Why does my thermostat say one temperature but the room feels different?

The thermostat may be in direct sun, near a lamp or draft, dusty, or low on batteries. Fix those first; if readings are still wrong, the thermostat or its sensor may need checking.

How do I know if my thermostat needs new batteries?

A blank or dim screen, or a system that stops responding, often means dead batteries. Replace them with fresh AA or AAA batteries as marked inside the cover.
