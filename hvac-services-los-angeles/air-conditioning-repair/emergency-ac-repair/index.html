Signs and causes

Common Causes

These are common causes, not a diagnosis. A technician must inspect the equipment.

Total cooling loss

The system runs or stops and the home keeps heating up.

Burning smell

Turn the system off at the thermostat and breaker. Call 911 if you see smoke or fire.

Active water leak

Turn the AC off to prevent damage to ceilings and floors.

Breaker keeps tripping

A repeating trip can mean a short, failed motor or compressor.

Safety: If you smell burning, see smoke or sparks, turn the system off and call 911 if there is fire. Never open electrical panels or handle refrigerant yourself.

Before the technician arrives

What to Check Before You Call

These steps need no tools and no opening of equipment panels.

  1. Turn the AC off at the thermostat if you see ice, water or smell burning.
  2. Do not reset the breaker more than once.
  3. Open windows and use fans for safety in extreme heat, and check on children, seniors and pets.
  4. Note any noises, error codes and when the problem started before calling.

Hiring an HVAC Contractor in Los Angeles

Verify the license

California HVAC work needs a CSLB C-20 license. Check the number at cslb.ca.gov.

Ask about permits

Replacing HVAC equipment in the City of Los Angeles generally requires a permit, and Title 24 may apply.

Get it in writing

Request an itemized written estimate that names parts, refrigerant and warranty terms.
