Common causes

Common Reasons a Furnace Will Not Turn On

Most no-start calls trace back to one of these. The cause is only confirmed after an assessment.

Safety first. If you smell gas, hear a hissing sound, or a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, leave the home. Call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside. Do not operate light switches, and do not try to troubleshoot gas equipment yourself.

Thermostat settings or batteries

Wrong mode, a hold setting, or dead batteries can stop the heat call.

Power interruption

A tripped breaker, off wall switch, or blown fuse cuts power to the furnace.

Clogged filter

Restricted airflow can trigger a safety shutdown or stop start-up.

Door switch not engaged

If the blower door is loose, the furnace may refuse to start for safety.

Ignition problem

A failed igniter or sensor can stop the start sequence.

Control board fault

The board that runs the start-up can fail, causing a no-start with no obvious cause.

These are common possibilities, not a diagnosis. The furnace needs an assessment to confirm what is wrong.

Before you call

Furnace Won't Turn On? 5 Safe Things to Check First

These checks need no tools and no opening of panels. If the furnace still does not start, call.

  1. Check the thermostat. Set to Heat, setpoint above room temperature, fresh batteries.
  2. Check the furnace wall switch. It should be in the on position.
  3. Check the breaker once. Reset it one time. If it trips again, stop and call.
  4. Check the air filter. Replace it if it is clogged.
  5. Smell gas? Leave the home. Call 911 or SoCalGas from outside. Do not touch switches.

Do not open or repair gas equipment yourself

Furnaces involve gas, high voltage, and moving parts. A furnace that trips a breaker or shuts down for safety should be checked, not forced to run.

  • Note what the thermostat shows
  • Note whether you hear any sounds when it tries to start
  • Note when it stopped working, and any recent power outage
  • Know the furnace type and approximate age, if you can

Repair vs. replacement

Should I Repair or Replace a Furnace That Will Not Start?

One failed part on a healthy furnace is a repair. Repeated no-start problems on an old furnace are a signal to price a replacement.

A common rule of thumb multiplies the furnace's age in years by the repair estimate. If the total is above $5,000, price a replacement. It is a guide, not a quote.

Be an informed homeowner

What to Ask Before You Approve Any Furnace Repair

Whichever company you choose, these questions help you understand the work and the cost before you agree to it.

What exactly failed?

Ask which part or control is the problem and why it caused your symptom. A clear explanation is a good sign.

Can I see it?

Ask to be shown the failed part or reading, especially for large findings such as a cracked heat exchanger.

Is there a written estimate?

Ask for the scope and total in writing before work begins, and what happens if the scope changes.

Is the diagnostic fee credited?

Many companies credit a diagnostic fee toward the repair, but not all do. Ask before the visit.

Is the contractor licensed?

HVAC work in California requires a CSLB C-20 license. Ask for the number and check it at cslb.ca.gov.

Does this fix the cause?

Ask whether the repair addresses the root cause or only the symptom, so the problem does not return.

Furnace Not Turning On Los Angeles: FAQs

Short answers to common questions. They are general information, not a diagnosis of your furnace.

Why won't my furnace turn on?

Common causes include thermostat settings or dead batteries, a tripped breaker or off switch, a clogged filter, an ignition problem, or a control fault. Check the thermostat, wall switch, breaker and filter. If it still will not start, call a professional.

Why is my furnace not turning on but the thermostat is on?

The thermostat may be sending a heat call that the furnace cannot act on because of a power, safety switch, ignition, or control problem. Check the furnace wall switch and breaker once, then request an assessment.

Where is the furnace reset switch?

Many furnaces have a wall switch that looks like a light switch near the unit, and some have a reset button inside the cabinet. Do not open gas equipment. Check the wall switch and breaker, then call.

Can a dirty filter stop a furnace from starting?

Yes. A clogged filter can restrict airflow enough to trigger a safety shutdown or stop start-up. Replace a dirty filter and try once.

What should I do if the breaker keeps tripping?

Stop resetting it and call. A breaker that trips again signals an electrical problem that needs a professional.

What if I smell gas?

Leave the home immediately. Call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside. Do not use light switches or try to relight the furnace.

How much does it cost to fix a furnace that won't start?

It depends on the cause. Published national figures put typical furnace repairs at about $150 to $600, with control boards around $300 to $650. Ask for a written estimate.
