What should I have ready when I call about service in my area?

Have your Los Angeles ZIP code and street address, whether the problem is heating or cooling, what the thermostat shows, when it started, and the approximate age of the equipment if known. Mention any unusual sounds or visible water.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the building immediately and call 911 or your gas utility from a safe place outside. Do not touch light switches or try to inspect gas equipment. Call an HVAC professional only after the gas utility says it is safe.

How can I check that an HVAC contractor is licensed in California?

HVAC work in California requires a CSLB C-20 license. Ask the contractor for the license number, then look it up on the Contractors State License Board website at cslb.ca.gov to confirm it is active.
