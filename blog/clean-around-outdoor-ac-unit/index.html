Clearing Leaves and Debris Around Your Outdoor AC Unit

Your outdoor AC unit needs air to breathe. Leaves, dust, garden cuttings and plants crowding it make it run hotter, cool less, and wear out sooner. Here is a ten-minute tidy-up anyone can do.

Quick answer

How Much Space Should I Leave Around My AC Unit?

Leave at least 2 feet (about 60 cm) of clear space on all sides of your outdoor AC unit, and keep plants trimmed back so nothing touches it. Clear leaves and debris from the top grille and the ground around it, and rinse light dust off the outside fins gently with a garden hose on low pressure.

Step by step

The 10-Minute Clear-Up

Turn the thermostat to off first. Then work around — never inside — the unit.

  1. Pick up leaves, twigs and garden cuttings from the top grille and the ground around the unit.
  2. Trim back plants and grass so there is at least 2 feet of clear space on every side.
  3. Gently hose light dust off the outside fins from top to bottom, on low pressure. Do not use a pressure washer.
  4. Check that the concrete pad is level and that water drains away from the unit, not toward the house.
  5. Turn the thermostat back on and listen: the fan should run smoothly without rattling.
What not to do

Do Not Open or Hose Inside the Unit

Never remove the fan grille or panels, never spray water into the electrical compartment, and never use a pressure washer — bent fins block airflow worse than dust does. Cleaning the inside coils and straightening fins is part of professional air conditioning cleaning. Your job is the outside: space, leaves and light dust.

Why it matters

What Blocked Airflow Costs You

A blocked condenser cannot release heat properly, so your AC runs longer, cools less, and uses more energy. In hot Los Angeles summers that extra strain is one of the things that leads to an AC that cannot keep up or a frozen coil. Ten minutes a month is a small habit that protects a large, expensive piece of equipment.

Stop and call a professional if…

Do not remove panels or the fan grille, do not touch wiring or the capacitor, and do not use a pressure washer. If the fan rattles, the unit hums without starting, or you see damaged wiring, call (213) 771-9223.

If you smell gas at any point, leave the home immediately and call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside. Do not use light switches or try to fix a gas issue yourself.

FAQs

Outdoor AC Unit Questions

How much clearance does an outdoor AC unit need?

At least 2 feet of clear space on all sides, with nothing touching the unit. More space is better on the side where the fan blows air out.

Can I hose down my AC unit?

Yes, gently. Use a garden hose on low pressure from top to bottom to rinse light dust off the outside fins. Never use a pressure washer and never spray into the electrical parts.

Should I cover my AC unit in winter?

In Los Angeles it is usually not needed. A cover can trap moisture and attract pests. Clearing leaves and debris is more useful than covering it.

Why is my AC running but not cooling well?

Blocked airflow around the outdoor unit is one possible cause, along with a dirty filter or low refrigerant. Clear the space around the unit and check the filter first; if cooling does not improve, call a professional.
