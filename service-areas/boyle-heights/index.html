Before the technician arrives

AC or Heater Stopped Working? 6 Safe Things to Check First

These checks need no tools and no opening of equipment panels.

  1. Check the thermostat. Confirm the mode (heat or cool), the set temperature and the batteries.
  2. Check the air filter. A clogged filter restricts airflow. Replace it if it is dirty.
  3. Open vents and return grilles. Furniture or closed registers can block airflow.
  4. Check the breaker once. If a breaker trips, reset it one time. If it trips again, stop and call.
  5. Look for ice or water. If you see either at the unit, turn the system off.
  6. Smell gas? Leave the home. Call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not touch switches.
