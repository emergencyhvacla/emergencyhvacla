Common causes

Why Furnace Blower Motors Fail

Blower motors run every heating and cooling cycle, and in many Los Angeles homes the same blower serves the air conditioner too. That adds up to a lot of running hours.

Safety first. If you smell gas, hear a hissing sound, or a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, leave the home. Call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside. Do not operate light switches, and do not try to troubleshoot gas equipment yourself.

Worn bearings

Long run hours wear bearings, causing squealing, grinding, or a motor that finally seizes.

Failed capacitor

On PSC motors, a weak capacitor can stop the motor from starting, causing humming with no spin.

Dirty blower wheel

Dust packs the wheel, strains the motor, and reduces airflow.

Clogged filter

A restricted filter makes the motor work harder and can trigger overheating shutdowns.

Control or relay fault

The motor may be fine while the board fails to power it.

Overheating

Heat from restricted airflow shortens motor life and can trip the high limit.

These are common possibilities, not a diagnosis. The furnace needs an assessment to confirm what is wrong.

Before you call

Blower Not Working? 5 Safe Things to Check First

These checks need no tools and no opening of panels. If the blower still does not behave, call.

  1. Check the thermostat fan setting. Set it to Auto. If it was On, the fan runs continuously, which is normal for that setting.
  2. Check the air filter. A clogged filter restricts airflow. Replace it if dirty.
  3. Open vents and registers. Blocked supply or return vents reduce airflow.
  4. Check the furnace switch and breaker once. If the breaker trips again, stop and call.
  5. Listen. Note humming, squealing, rattling, or silence and tell us when you call.

Do not open the furnace to inspect the blower

Blower motors connect to high voltage, capacitors that can hold a charge, and spinning parts. Leave inspection and repair to qualified professionals.

  • Note the sound the blower makes, if any
  • Note whether warm air ever comes out
  • Note when the problem started
  • Know the furnace brand and approximate age, if you can

Repair vs. replacement

Should I Repair or Replace a Furnace With a Failing Blower Motor?

A new motor on a furnace in good shape can add years of use. On an old furnace, a costly motor may be the nudge toward replacement.

A common rule of thumb multiplies the furnace's age in years by the repair estimate. If the total is above $5,000, price a replacement. It is a guide, not a quote.

Be an informed homeowner

What to Ask Before You Approve Any Furnace Repair

Whichever company you choose, these questions help you understand the work and the cost before you agree to it.

What exactly failed?

Ask which part or control is the problem and why it caused your symptom. A clear explanation is a good sign.

Can I see it?

Ask to be shown the failed part or reading, especially for large findings such as a cracked heat exchanger.

Is there a written estimate?

Ask for the scope and total in writing before work begins, and what happens if the scope changes.

Is the diagnostic fee credited?

Many companies credit a diagnostic fee toward the repair, but not all do. Ask before the visit.

Is the contractor licensed?

HVAC work in California requires a CSLB C-20 license. Ask for the number and check it at cslb.ca.gov.

Does this fix the cause?

Ask whether the repair addresses the root cause or only the symptom, so the problem does not return.

Furnace Blower Motor Repair Los Angeles: FAQs

Short answers to common questions. They are general information, not a diagnosis of your furnace.

Why is my furnace blower humming but not spinning?

A humming motor that does not turn often points to a failed capacitor, seized bearings, or a motor that cannot start. Turn the furnace off to avoid damage and call a technician.

What does a furnace blower motor do?

The blower motor spins the fan that pushes air through the furnace and out the vents. Without it, warm air stays in the furnace and the furnace may shut down on overheating.

How much does blower motor replacement cost?

Published national figures put furnace blower motor replacement at about $300 to $900, with an average near $560. Variable-speed ECM motors can run higher. Ask for a written estimate.

What are signs of a bad blower motor?

Common signs include weak airflow, humming, squealing or grinding noises, a furnace that heats but pushes no air, and overheating shutdowns. These can have other causes, so an assessment is needed.

Can a furnace run without a blower motor?

It should not. Without airflow the furnace can overheat and shut down on its safety switch. Keep the furnace off and call a technician.

Is the problem the motor or the capacitor?

It can be either. On PSC motors a failed capacitor often mimics a dead motor and is a smaller repair. A technician needs to test it.

Why does my furnace blower run all the time?

Often the thermostat fan is set to On. Set it to Auto. If it still runs constantly, a relay or control problem may be involved and you should request an assessment.

Should I repair or replace a furnace with a bad blower motor?
