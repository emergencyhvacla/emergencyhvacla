Questions to Ask Any HVAC Company Before You Approve Work

Homeowners often worry about surprise bills and pressure to replace. These questions help you stay in control with any provider.

  1. What exactly is wrong?Ask for the diagnosis in plain language and which part failed.
  2. Is the estimate in writing?Get the scope, parts and total before work begins.
  3. Is repair an option?Ask for repair and replacement choices side by side.
  4. Can I verify the license?California contractors are listed in the CSLB license lookup.
  5. What does the warranty cover?Ask about parts and labor separately.
  6. Are rebates available?Programs such as TECH Clean California may apply to some replacements. Check eligibility before buying.

Should I repair or replace my furnace or AC?

Repair usually suits a single, isolated fault. Replacement is worth discussing when a system is old, fails repeatedly, or repair costs approach the price of new equipment. Ask for both options in writing and compare them before deciding.

What is the difference between HVAC cleaning and a repair?

Cleaning is routine care for a system that still runs. Repair fixes a specific fault such as no heat, no cooling or unusual noise. Replacement installs new equipment when the old system is no longer practical to keep fixing.
