Your AC or Furnace Won't Turn On? Check These First

Nothing happens when you turn the system on. No fan, no hum, nothing. Before you assume the worst, check the three things that cause most no-power calls — they take two minutes and need no tools beyond fresh batteries.

Quick answer

Why Won't My AC or Furnace Turn On at All?

The most common causes are a thermostat with dead batteries or the wrong mode, a tripped circuit breaker, or the furnace power switch turned off. Check the thermostat mode and batteries, the breaker panel, and the power switch near the furnace. If all three are fine and nothing happens, the problem is inside the system and needs a professional.

The three checks

Check These in Order

  1. Thermostat: is it on the right mode — Cool in summer, Heat in winter — and set beyond the room temperature? If the screen is blank or dim, put in fresh batteries.
  2. Breaker: open the electrical panel and look for the breaker labeled furnace, AC, or air handler. If it is tripped (in the middle or off position), switch it fully off, then back on — once.
  3. Power switch: near the furnace there is usually a switch that looks like a light switch. Make sure it is on. In some homes it gets bumped off by accident.

After each step, wait a minute and listen for the fan or the system starting. If it starts, you found it. If nothing happens after all three, stop there — the fault is inside the equipment.

What the sounds mean

Humming, Clicking, or Silence?

Silence usually points to power, thermostat, or a failed control. A hum with no fan can mean a failed capacitor or motor — turn it off and call. Repeated clicking with no start often means an ignition or control problem on a furnace. Do not keep trying to force it on; repeated attempts can damage parts. Our AC not turning on and furnace not turning on pages explain what a technician tests next.

One reset only

Do Not Keep Resetting the Breaker

If the breaker trips again right after you reset it, leave it off and call (213) 771-9223. A breaker that keeps tripping is protecting you from a short or a failing motor, and forcing it back on risks damage — or worse.

Stop and call a professional if…

Do not open the electrical panel beyond the breaker switches, do not touch wiring, the capacitor, or the igniter, and do not remove furnace panels. If you see sparking, smell burning, or the breaker trips repeatedly, leave it off and call (213) 771-9223.

If you smell gas at any point, leave the home immediately and call 911 or SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside. Do not use light switches or try to fix a gas issue yourself.

FAQs

System Will Not Turn On Questions

Why will my AC not turn on at all?

Check the thermostat mode and batteries, the circuit breaker, and the furnace or air-handler power switch. If all three are fine, the fault is inside the system.

My thermostat screen is blank. Is the thermostat broken?

Not usually — dead batteries are the most common cause. Try fresh batteries first.

Should I reset a tripped breaker more than once?

No. Reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off and call a professional.

The system hums but the fan does not start. What is wrong?

That can point to a failed capacitor or motor. Turn the system off and call a professional rather than letting it hum.
